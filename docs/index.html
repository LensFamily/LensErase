EVALUATING THE INTENDED EDIT

LensErase-Bench

Removal can have more than one valid completion. Our 500-case benchmark evaluates outputs from the source image and mask, without requiring a paired ground-truth target.

Removal

Complete removal of the targets and associated effects.
